Perler Bead · How to play

A two-person, multi-round communication task. The Speaker can see the source image and describes it in words, round by round. The Listener cannot see the source image and places beads on a blank grid from the description. When both people end communication, the image is revealed and they create a final 12×12 pair-consensus version together.
1Speaker looks at the source image, writes one description, clicks "Send instruction", then draws the board they expect the Listener to build from it.
2Listener reads the instruction, clicks grid cells to place beads (click again = remove), then clicks "Submit round".
3Only now does the Speaker see the Listener's submitted board alongside their own forecast—without any answer-position highlights—and sends the next round.
4Either person can request the end. After the other confirms, communication is frozen, the Listener sees the source image, and both edit a new blank grid until they confirm the pair-consensus result.
