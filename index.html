Perler Bead · How to play

A two-person, multi-round communication task. The Speaker can see the target pattern and describes it in words, round by round. The Listener cannot see the target and places beads on a blank grid from the description. The goal: reconstruct the target in as few rounds as possible.
1Speaker looks at the target on the left, writes one description, and clicks "Send instruction".
2Listener reads the instruction, clicks grid cells to place beads (click again = remove), then clicks "Submit round".
3Only now does the Speaker see the Listener's progress (red = extra / outline = missing) and sends the next round. Repeat until reconstructed.
